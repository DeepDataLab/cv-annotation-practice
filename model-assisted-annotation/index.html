Pre-label confidence explorer

YOLOv8n pre-labels on 8 frames of a busy night street video, compared with my own from-scratch annotation of the same frames. Move the slider to see what happens if only boxes above a confidence threshold are sent for review.

Trade-off: a higher threshold means fewer boxes to check and a higher share of kept boxes that I also drew, but more of my own boxes end up without any model box. At the highest thresholds only a few boxes remain, so those values are noisy.

Small experiment: 8 frames, one annotator. A pattern, not proof. Same place means IoU of at least 0.5, class may differ.
